# NEXUS GENESIS

Date: 2026-03-09

Protocol: NEXUS FINANCE

## Mission

NEXUS is a sovereign credit protocol designed to issue a stable credit unit (NXUSD)
through deterministic financial rules and stress-tested risk systems.

## Core Components

- NXUSD stable credit asset
- VaultManager collateralized credit engine
- LiquidationEngine safety layer
- OracleModule price feed system
- Economic Constitution
- Treasury Receipt Engine
- Sovereign Multisig Governance

## Governance Topology

Admin: Safe Multisig (4/7)

Guardian: protocol emergency authority

Keeper: liquidation executor

## Genesis Objective

Bootstrapping the NXUSD credit system and activating the NEXUS financial network.

## Long-Term Vision

NEXUS aims to become the programmable credit layer of the crypto economy.
