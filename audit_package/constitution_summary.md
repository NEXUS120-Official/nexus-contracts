# Economic Constitution

# NEXUS ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.0

Defines immutable economic invariants for the Nexus protocol.

## Invariants

### Collateral Ratio

Minimum collateralization:

150%

The protocol must always maintain a minimum collateral ratio above this level.

### NXUSD Supply Cap

Maximum total supply:

1,000,000,000 NXUSD

Prevents uncontrolled expansion of the stable supply.

## Governance constraint

Governance actions that violate these invariants are considered invalid by design.

## Purpose

The economic constitution provides:

- deterministic safety rules
- audit verifiability
- governance constraint layer
